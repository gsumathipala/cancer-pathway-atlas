Tissue context

Pathway use by tumour type

Qualitative tiers synthesised from the TCGA pathway paper [1] and the tumour-type marker papers. Stated anchors: melanoma 95%, thyroid 84%, pancreas 78%, lung adenocarcinoma 74% RTK–RAS; lung squamous 68% and non-hypermutated endometrial up to 95% PI3K; NRF2 25% in lung squamous [1]. The same pathway is often hit through different genes in different tissues (for example KRAS in pancreas, BRAF in thyroid, EGFR in glioblastoma), which reflects cell of origin [15].

Ten principles that organise the cancer genome

    Scope and limits. Frequencies come from primary-tumour cohorts (mostly TCGA), which under-represent metastatic, paediatric, haematological and non-European-ancestry cancers. Purity limits detection of deletions, so suppressor loss (for example CDKN2A in pancreas) is undercounted. Gene-level percentages in this atlas are rounded guides for comparison and should not be cited as point estimates. Drug names reflect approvals and trials up to 2026 and are not treatment advice.
